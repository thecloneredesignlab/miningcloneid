One correction: only buffer_smax is approximately equal across all three families. n_O is equal in C01/C02 but
  higher in vivo in C03; p_mis_base is equal in C01 but higher in vivo in C02/C03.

  More importantly, equality of one parameter does not imply equality of the complete biological function.

  The survival parameters are particularly informative jointly. With similar buffer_smax but higher buffer_beta in
  vitro, survival near 2N can be lower while high-ploidy cells approach the shared ceiling more closely. Together
  with buffer_n_exp, this can produce the function-level result already identified: tumors have a higher survival
  floor, whereas culture has a steeper ploidy-dependent survival slope.

  ────────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   buffer_n_exp              The chromosome-number dependence of post-missegregation survival is shaped more sharply

  The simultaneous increase in p_misseg and k_o_mis is not contradictory. In the model,

  # [
  p_{\mathrm{mis,eff}}

  p_{\mathrm{mis,base}}
  +
  p_{\mathrm{misseg}}
  \frac{\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}+k_{\mathrm{o,mis}}}.
  ]

  The tumor fits therefore encode a larger but more stress-gated CIN response. Because mu_hp and gamma_mu are also
  higher in vivo, severely stressed tumor cells can nevertheless reach that elevated CIN regime.

  Across All Families
  The most defensible joint interpretation is:

  1. Culture supports a higher proliferative ceiling.
  2. Tumors translate resource stress more strongly into ploidy-dependent growth suppression, death, and inducible
     missegregation.

  3. The maximum buffering ceiling is shared, but the shape of post-missegregation survival differs.
  4. The families disagree on whether baseline CIN and oxygen-response steepness must additionally differ.
  5. They disagree even more strongly about WGD probability, so no context-wide directional claim about p_wgd is
     justified.

  Thus, the common biological story is not that tumors necessarily possess fundamentally different baseline
  chromosome-segregation machinery. The more robust difference is in how experienced stress is converted into
  selection and CIN. Higher baseline CIN, sharper oxygen response, and altered WGD generation are alternative family-
  specific refinements rather than requirements shared by every good-fitting regime.
