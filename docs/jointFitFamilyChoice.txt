  Current interpretation

  - C01 has the best single objective.
  - C01 and C02 are effectively tied. C02’s alternative pair, C02Sc02, reaches 18.890, only 0.038 or 0.20% above C01. Moreover,
    C01’s advantage over the primary C02 pair is primarily its smaller coupling penalty; without that penalty, C02Sc01 is
    actually slightly better.

  - C02 is the most robust family to representative choice. Its two pair minima are 18.890 and 18.970. C01’s are 18.852 and
    19.791, while C03’s are 19.414 and 19.978. Thus C01 has the best individual solution, but C02 performs consistently well from
    both representatives.

  - C03 is measurably worse under the current objective, mostly because of poorer terminal-ploidy and necrosis fit. It fits tumor
    burden slightly better than C01/C02, so it is not dominated on every modality.

  The pair-level values are in revised/iteration2/manuscript/tables/supp_joint_search_summary.tex:12.

  I would therefore describe C01 and C02 as the leading fit tier, with C02 the more initialization-robust family, and C03 as a
  lower-fitting but biologically distinct alternative.

  How To Choose

  1. Held-out predictive performance: This should be decisive. Use leave-one-mouse/harvest-out prediction in vivo and hold out an
     in-vitro lineage, passage interval, or late measurement. Compare predictive log score using the existing observation models.

  2. Data-only fit by modality: Compare burden, terminal ploidy, necrosis, growth, karyotype, and flow separately, excluding
     priors and coupling penalties. Avoid allowing one weighted component to determine the result invisibly.

  3. Regularization sensitivity: Repeat or rescore across reasonable coupling strengths, prior widths, and modality weights. The
     small C01–C02 difference could readily reverse.

  4. Search stability: Prefer a family that remains competitive across both representatives, repeated broad initializations, and
     deduplicated endpoints without heavy bound dependence. This currently favors C02.

  5. Independent biological predictions: Predefine predictions that differ among families, then test them using longitudinal CIN,
     ploidy, necrosis, or oxygen measurements. For example, the fitted coupled oxygen–ploidy trajectory increases for C03Sc02 but
     decreases for the displayed C01 and C02 pairs; however, that sign also changes between representatives within C02/C03, so it
     is not yet a stable family property. See revised/iteration2/manuscript/tables/supp_joint_fixed_o2_summary.tex:10.

  6. Numerical reliability of extrapolation: C01’s separate-fit representative is classified as spectrally unreliable, while C02
     and C03 are classified as caution rather than reliable. This matters for fixed-O₂ asymptotic predictions, not for
     calibration fit itself.

  AIC/BIC or significance tests on the 500 optimizer seeds would not be appropriate: the families have the same complexity, and
  optimizer starts are search diagnostics, not independent samples. Until held-out data distinguish them, the defensible
  manuscript strategy is to retain all three, emphasize conclusions shared across families, and treat family-specific behavior as
  unresolved model uncertainty.
