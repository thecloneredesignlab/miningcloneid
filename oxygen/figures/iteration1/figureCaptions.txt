\caption{\textbf{In vitro ploidy reshaping occurs without strong high-ploidy elimination.}
\textbf{(A) In vitro fit summary.} Panel file: \texttt{fig3a\_invitro\_growth\_ploidy\_burden\_fit.png}. This panel was extracted from \texttt{in\_vitro\_fit\_report\_seed10.html} and aligns growth-rate observations, chromosome-count observations, and model-predicted live/dead burden components for 2N and 4N control and oxygen-deprived branches. It supports the intended fit-summary panel by showing that the culture fit jointly tracks proliferation and ploidy across passages rather than fitting either stream alone.
\textbf{(B) Fig. 3B: missing. Missegregation source.} The intended panel should show that high-ploidy cells missegregate more often. The removed nonviable-daughter diagnostic was mechanistically related but did not directly visualize this quantity.
\textbf{(C) Survival buffering.} Panel file: \texttt{fig3c\_invitro\_viability\_after\_missegregation.png}. This report panel shows model-implied viability after a one-copy missegregation event as a function of chromosome number. The increasing curve visualizes the fitted buffering mechanism, in which higher-ploidy states better tolerate missegregation products.
\textbf{(D) Fig. 3D: missing. Downward reshaping.} The intended panel should show surviving chromosome-loss daughters generating viable lower-ploidy descendants. The removed distribution heatmap showed ploidy reshaping over time but did not directly show this cellular route.
\textbf{(E) Fig. 3E: missing. Negative control / rejected interpretation.} The intended panel should make clear that the in vitro explanation is not simply that 4N cells are killed off. No current iteration-1 panel directly tests or visualizes that rejected interpretation.}
\label{fig:iteration1-invitro-ploidy-reshaping}
\end{figure}

\caption{\textbf{In vivo fit-derived resource regimes and parameter landscapes.}
\textbf{(A) Oxygen framing.} Panel file: \texttt{fig4a\_invivo\_effective\_oxygen\_trajectories.png}. This in vivo report panel shows fitted effective O$_2$ trajectories for the separate in vivo seed 146 fit. It is retained as partial evidence for the intended oxygen/resource framing, but should be interpreted as a model-implied latent resource variable rather than direct oxygen measurement or a complete representation of tumor resource stress.
\textbf{(B) Low-O$_2$ regime.} Panel file: \texttt{fig4b\_low\_o2\_main\_feature\_auc.png}. The low-reference-O$_2$ feature-AUC panel from the mode-parameter contribution report summarizes which fitted parameters best discriminate ploidy mode under severe resource stress. It is a first-pass classifier-based representation of the intended low-O$_2$ regime, where stress-associated death/growth pressure is expected to dominate.
\textbf{(C) Intermediate-O$_2$ regime.} Panel file: \texttt{fig4c\_intermediate\_o2\_main\_feature\_auc.png}. The O$_2$=1 feature-AUC panel is retained as the intermediate-regime candidate because it shows a more mixed set of discriminating features, consistent with the intended claim that intermediate O$_2$ is tradeoff-dependent and less reducible to a single parameter.
\textbf{(D) High-O$_2$ regime.} Panel file: \texttt{fig4d\_high\_o2\_main\_feature\_auc.png}. The high-reference-O$_2$ feature-AUC panel summarizes discriminating features when death pressure is reduced, including baseline missegregation and buffering-related terms.
\textbf{(E) Parameter landscape.} Panel file: \texttt{fig4e\_landscape\_cluster\_driver\_violins.png}. This paired violin plot shows prior-aware z positions for \texttt{p\_mis\_base} and \texttt{n\_O} across in vivo t-SNE clusters. It is retained because it directly connects fitted solution regions to interpretable biological axes and better matches the intended landscape panel than the removed embedding-only t-SNE plot.}
