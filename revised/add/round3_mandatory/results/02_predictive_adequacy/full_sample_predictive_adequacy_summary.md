# Full-sample predictive adequacy

Standalone in-vitro diagnostics include 114 growth records, 220 directly karyotyped cells across 12 passages, and 20 flow-density samples.
Across the six frozen joint winners, burden diagnostics include 92 nonzero tumor-timepoints from 8 tumors and terminal-ploidy diagnostics include 8 tumors.
Observed burden falls inside the six-winner min-max envelope at 14/92 timepoints; observed terminal mean chromosome state falls inside the corresponding envelope for 4/8 tumors.

These are in-sample predictive checks. The six-winner ranges reflect numerical solution multiplicity, not sampling uncertainty. Leave-one-tumor-out or leave-one-lineage-out validation requires refitting and is not claimed here.
