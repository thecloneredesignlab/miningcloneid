## Selected composition

- **A, Death-linked CIN creates an adaptive feedback.** Replaces the prior
  overview with the approved feedback schematic. It separates environmental
  O2 from chromosome-state-specific experienced stress, routes death hazard to
  the effective missegregation probability, retains WGD as a separate source of
  variation, and returns adapted composition to population-average death.
- **B, Direct fitness effects of chromosome burden.** Retains the prior
  qualitative proliferation/death curves but removes the outcome claim that low
  O2 necessarily selects a particular endpoint. Its role is limited to the
  encoded direct fitness cost that makes chromosome number selectable.
- **C, Ploidy-dependent survival after chromosome missegregation.** Retains the
  correlated daughter mechanism while removing the duplicated death-to-CIN and
  WGD callouts. The higher- and lower-N rows now compare the same absolute
  chromosome shift and use distinct daughter chromosome counts.

## Layout decision

Panel A occupies the full upper row. Panels B and C form the lower row, with C
receiving more width because its correlated-daughter structure requires four
cell glyphs and row-level survival annotations. The final output is 7.1 by 6.35
inches, with a 2130 by 1905 pixel review PNG at 300 dpi and a vector PDF.
